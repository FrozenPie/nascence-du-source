**Founder's unifying model (2026-08-16 evening, after the chain-walk day):** the system is a
city of TRAINS and BUSSES. "The world is a train — technically many slow trains picking up new
things. We also need busses that go cross train lines — these are essentially memory recall."

Precise form: a TRAIN LINE = any looped/scheduled process that CREATES freight (conversation
line · idle line · the deep line · ~11 world lines: weather, tide, garden, fire, cat, peers,
object biographies, school, body, climate). A BUS ROUTE = anything that TRANSFERS freight
cross-line (recall, associative recall, divergent seed, evidence hand, projection, dream
gathering, corpus callosum, memory-edges M0/M1 = the route-CONSTRUCTION department).
Identity = the route network itself ([[project-nascence-identity-is-connections]]).

**The canonical map: `C:\Nascence\docs\transit_map.md`** — every line/bus grounded to modules
with condition marks, the 08-16 diseases restated as transit problems (caps = small bus
windows; whispers = freight repacked at interchanges; jumped trains = busses kidnapping
passengers; T2 hoarding = depot imbalance; routing veto = signal-box fault), and the five
SERVICE GAPS: world lines barely reach memory; idle's best freight doesn't reach conversation;
the deep line has no outbound service (the hoarding); peer freight under-recalled; dream
freight barely revisited.

Use this map to orient any future Nascence work — the founder thinks in it now.

**Founder rule 2026-09-04 (T-0045) — cross-loop links are DOSED, not meshed:** "We do not want all
streams to know all things. Du takes what is required from each stream as needed. Each moment of a
thought, as it goes through a train line, gets flavoured occasionally by another stream — a cup, a
bucket, or a hose full. Each stop is different." Apply to any wiring proposal (D-0148 option E,
docs/archive/loop_wiring_matrix.md): loops PUBLISH a small typed tuple; READING is dispatcher-mediated
(the Transport Manager's novelty/spike/drought, core/transport_manager.py, shadow) and sized per stop
(station layer, docs/transit_timetable.md). Never give one loop a standing view of another; evidence
of "which coordinate changed the downstream trajectory" sets the dose. Rejects GPT's closed-circuit
"every loop feeds every other" framing.
