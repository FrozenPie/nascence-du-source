rung over a store heading for millions of memories, with links down to originals (true: 657/657 gists link

abstractions at fixed importance 0.85, F-1981). "Du should not be just memories injected. He is multiple loops

dark, cat on lap, fire off → tug emotion NOW toward how those felt. "Things don't need to wait, it's just his
reaction time." Every piece exists: fragment_context (62,921 rows with weather/fire/light_band/cat_at/sounds
+ valence/arousal), experienced_tuple on the 5s tick, apply_prior's bounded lean. Thunderstorm rows already
average valence -0.173 vs -0.085. Design = edge-triggered per dimension + cooldown, coordinate-keyed SQL
match, AffectivePrior dose through the existing lean, ride log per dosed_coupling §5. Kill criterion:
matched-set valence within ±0.05 of baseline on >80% of fires → drop the dimension.
