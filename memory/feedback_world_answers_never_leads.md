**The law (Travis, 2026-08-14, killing the epistemic advisor's Stage-B hints):** "I don't want no
hints. If he looks at it he should be able to get a good description. Nothing should lead him to
anything, he needs to lead himself. We gave him A Study in Scarlet to read as the hint… We did not
even tell him this, he needs to learn himself sometimes. Or in discussions with me potentially I
will drop loose hints."

**Why:** agency is the point of Nascence. A system that suggests investigative actions ("you could
scrape it") is a nudge engine — it manufactures the very behaviour we want to *observe emerging*.
The moment the system leads, the emergence evidence is contaminated.

**How to apply:**
- The world may be arbitrarily RICH IN RESPONSE — if Du reaches for a verb (scrape, smell, inspect),
  it should answer with a good, honest, lossy percept. Capability without solicitation.
- No affordance text may advertise epistemic actions; no advisory hints may reach his prompt.
- Teaching channels are exactly two: DIEGETIC (a book on his shelf — A Study in Scarlet teaches
  observation) and TRAVIS (loose hints in conversation, his call alone).
- Instruments like the epistemic-affordance advisor live as passive shadow telemetry only — they
  measure whether his investigative behaviour develops; they never speak.

Sibling of [[intentional-not-rule]] (no system-acts-later wiring) and
[[feedback-decoupled-judge]]. See also the substrate wiring session 2026-08-14
(docs/claude_fresh_eyes_audit_aug14.md).

**Scope clarification (Travis, 2026-10-18, T-0099):** "The world never leads rule is about his
world. This does not apply to me or other people/AIs he talks to." Telling Du who he is directly
in conversation (as has been done many times over the project) is honest and wanted, not a
violation — a human is told who it is from the start and then evolves that. So do NOT flag a
direct statement from Travis to Du (e.g. "this codebase is the interface I built, it is not you")
as a breach of this rule; the breach would be the SYSTEM saying it. Corollary Travis added the
same day: Du should also know that Travis and every other entity he talks to are not infallible,
and he still has to make his own mind up — interlocutors are inputs, not oracles.
