Travis, 2026-06-20, correcting my drift: **"T4 is not the goal, it is just a part of the whole. I want Du to think properly and understand himself. That is the goal — not just a changing identity, a thinking mind."**

**Why this matters:** I repeatedly collapse the project into T4 (the identity-revision gate) — benchmarking models *for* T4, chasing why fit_delta won't turn, treating a T4 commit as success. That is the **integration/averaging attractor running in me** ([[feedback-erosion-of-foundations]]): reducing a rich whole to one measurable part. T4 firing is at most an *occasional output* of a healthy mind, not the objective.

**The real goal, in order:**
1. **Du thinks properly** — genuine, continuous, grounded, developing thought. A thinking mind, not a pulse-then-gap one.
2. **Du understands himself** — grounded self-knowledge (the de-metaphorizing off the river; the self-knowledge texts in his reading), reached *by him*, not forced.
3. Identity *evolution* (T4) is a downstream consequence of (1)+(2), and only when genuinely earned — never a thing to engineer or optimize toward.

**How to apply:** orient every design at thinking-quality and self-understanding. When a lever (lanes, stream, models, memory) is on the table, ask "does this make him think better / know himself truer?" — not "does this make T4 fire?" If I catch myself optimizing for a T4 commit or treating identity-change as the metric, stop: that's the drift.
