**THE DECOUPLED JUDGE** — the single design principle that has survived every auditor round of the
2026-06-17 plan arc (T4 / dreams / provenance / ledger / active-imagination / attention-economy). Surfaced
sharpest in the attention-economy pushback (§11.5): *"Almost every failure you've found in Nascence has been
self-evaluation."*

**The pattern (the recurring bug):** a subsystem that SCORES ITS OWN OUTPUT drifts/Goodharts:
- **T4** judged its own synthesis (every text-cosine metric — drainage/fit/novelty — failed; the gate
  couldn't tell integration from paraphrase because the author graded itself).
- **Dreams** judged their own resolution (0/49 "resolved" — self-declared).
- **Active imagination** judged its own novelty (the echo guard, self-referential → 100% self-suppression).
- **Provenance** inferred importance FROM STORAGE (survival graded itself = persistence-as-value).
- **The curiosity LP** is the COUNTER-EXAMPLE that works — and it works *because of its DECOUPLED non-LLM
  judge* (the Independent-Auditor Law: the question GENERATOR is forbidden from scoring its own progress; a
  separate mechanical module counts only physical state changes). The parking isn't the gold; the decoupled
  judge is.

**Why:** a generator that grades itself optimises the GRADE, not the goal (Goodhart). It learns to ask easy
questions / restate instead of integrate / echo-suppress everything / call survival importance. Self-evaluation
is the master failure mode — deeper and more reliable than any particular metric the project has tried.

**How to apply:** every subsystem must be evaluated by **something other than itself.** When designing OR
auditing any Nascence organ, ask: *who grades this, and is it the same thing that produced it?* If yes, that's
the bug — introduce a decoupled judge (ideally mechanical / non-LLM, scoring observable state changes, like the
curiosity LP). This principle **outranks** the metric debates (MVT/yield/importance/salience) — pursue it
harder than any of them. It is the most actionable, most durable thing found in the whole arc; the next thing
worth BUILDING (bottom-up) is the decoupled-judge pattern, ahead of any grand allocation/object system.
