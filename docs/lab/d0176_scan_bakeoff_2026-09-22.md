## How to read the two scoring axes

* **Agreement with the ledger's own verdicts is circular for the incumbent** (F-0948: the ledger's verdicts ARE qwen3.5:9b's), so the baseline's own re-run agreement is the test-retest CEILING, not an accuracy. A candidate that disagrees with the history is not thereby wrong: the ledger's pass rate is 86 to 92 percent of detections, and F-0055 and F-0769 already flagged that the rewritten prompt forms more stands, not fewer.
* The non-circular checks are the synthetic controls (known answers built from the prompt's own categories), the F-0677 physical-exclusion contrast (a mechanical regex, independent of the incumbent's verdict), the JSON-contract compliance, and same-input consistency (identical prompt, repeated).

## Reading

(1) The current seat is noisy on its own: on 40 percent (16 of 40) of identical real inputs it flips formed/not formed between three identical runs, and the templated ask-the-library string is formed 2 and rejected 3 times in 5 identical prompts, which is F-0770's failure mode reproduced on qwen3.5:9b. At temperature 0 all of that vanishes (0/20 flips, byte-identical outputs; F-1693), so it is sampling noise, curable at the call with no model change; a residual context sensitivity remains (the same thought under a different real active list flips the gate in 6/32 swaps at temperature 0).

(2) The ledger itself is lenient. I read all 96 thoughts against the prompt's own categories (inferred, one reader): none holds a clear stand (0 clear, 7 ambiguous, 37 chore-like, 38 description or musing, 14 idle wants), yet the ledger formed 53 percent of the blind non-stand thoughts (36/68). So agreement with the ledger's history is a weak yardstick and the baseline's own re-run agreement (67 percent) is only a test-retest ceiling; the hold-rate north-star is computed over formed stands that, on this read, come almost entirely from thoughts holding no clear stand (of the 60 historically formed cases in the sample: 55 chore-like, descriptive or idle-want thoughts, 5 ambiguous, none a clear stand).

(3) granite3.1-moe:3b is unfit: it fails the controls, leaves the load_bearing flag out in 46 percent of its stands, almost never says false, never returns null, copies the whole thought as the stand in half its reports, and is as inconsistent as the baseline (18/40 gate flips, 33/40 full-verdict flips); this matches sitting's own granite audition.

(4) gemma4:12b is FIT for the task but is not shown to be MORE ACCURATE than the incumbent: it follows the contract (controls 24/24, no missing flags), is far more stable on identical input at the production temperature, rejects every one of the three templated strings in every run (0 formed of 27, including 0 of 12 under four different real active lists, against the baseline's 5 formed of 27; p=0.05), and rejects the F-0677 narrow-bucket regex set more often (62 vs 21 percent, p=0.008); but on my blind read it forms somewhat fewer non-stands than the baseline (40 vs 52 percent, not significant) and exactly as many chore-like thoughts (46 percent each). Its costs: it forms fewer stands (33 percent of the 96 sampled cases against 56 percent for the baseline's re-run; 48 against 60 percent weighted to the natural stream, so roughly a fifth to two-fifths fewer; 43 percent recall against the ledger; null on 55 percent of runs), it is a CPU model (median 21 s, p90 48 s: fine for a fire-and-forget scan on a 60 s cooldown, but the supervisor launches its hub lazy with idle-unload 300 s, so at about 263 scans a day, with gaps of several minutes, it would often meet a cold load), and its family (Google) differs from the qwen generator.

(5) command-r7b: no classification evidence, and on availability alone it is the weakest named seat.

## Caveats (read before quoting any number)

* **Circularity.** The ledger's verdicts are the current seat's (F-0948). Agreement with them is a test-retest ceiling for the baseline and a similarity measure for the candidates, not an accuracy.
* **The independent read is one reader's.** The S/C/D/W/A labels in `d0176_scan_bakeoff_data/labels_*.json` are the lab agent's own read of each thought against `_SCAN_SYS`'s categories: confidence `inferred`. Every rate quoted from them uses only the 73 labels written BEFORE any model output for that case had been seen (the 48 extension-sample cases and 25 main-sample cases); the other 23 main-sample labels were written after an early qualitative dump had shown one model's output for the case (they are the cases that dump listed as gemma4:12b's misses, a selected set), so they are excluded from rates and kept only for the class counts. The case id leaks the stratum prefix (xobs, xnar, xphy, xoth). Zero clear stands (S) were found in the 96 real thoughts, so recall on real stands could not be measured from real data: it rests on the 12 synthetic controls, which are easy (the baseline scores 24/24) and therefore only screen out models that cannot follow the prompt.
* **Reconstruction approximations** (see F-1691): the active list is a ledger replay (`survived N scans` approximated; two orphaned stands treated as ghosts); the `reaffirm` path (embedding match of a re-taken stand against the active list) is not simulated, so a candidate stand that production would have reaffirmed is counted here as formed; scans in which the incumbent hallucinated a statement that is not in the thought (5) are excluded by the join check; the 7 mechanical rejections (question, empty, too short) are excluded from the discrimination strata.
* **Sample sizes.** 96 real cases per model where the run completed (48 main + 48 extension), 40 repeated inputs (3 runs each), 12 controls x 2, 3 templated strings x 5. The physical-share estimate is post-stratified from 16 to 24 cases per stratum and is indicative only. Wilson 95 percent intervals are shown throughout; the repeated runs of one input are not independent draws, and the intervals treat inputs (not runs) as the unit.
* **Latency is not clean.** :11433 was shared with two other labs, :11437 with another lab; only :11439 was mine alone.
* **One attempt per unit.** Production retries a parse failure up to twice (`call_node_json`'s default); this harness scores the first attempt. Every JSON contract failure that matters here is schema-level (a missing `load_bearing`), which a retry does not cure.
* **command-r7b was not tested.**
