# J-3496 broadcast effect read (lexical, offline, no model judge)
Script: scripts/lab_broadcast_effect_J-3496.py. Data: 229 broadcast rows (218 principle-voiced, 8 self-claim, 3 corrected-turn); 180 joined (pivot thought within 30 min); 1145 matched controls (same 6h band, no consume in prior 30 min, paired with a same-band broadcast text). Measure: content-word Jaccard of next 3 thoughts vs broadcast text.
- principle-voiced (n=169): post overlap 0.0257 vs control 0.0093 (diff +0.0164, CI 0.0125..0.0205); post-minus-pre delta +0.0089 vs control -0.0005 (diff +0.0095, CI 0.0054..0.0136).
- Pre-overlap is already higher than control (0.0167 vs 0.0098): the broadcast text is derived from the thoughts just before it, so part of the post overlap is topic persistence, not effect. Only the delta partly controls this.
- Continuity with the preceding thoughts drops after a consume (prev/next Jaccard 0.076 vs 0.086, CI -0.016..-0.003): small topic shift. about-diversity unchanged in practice (2.96 vs 2.93).
- self-claim (n=8, delta +0.007) and corrected-turn (n=3, delta +0.019): descriptive only.
Reading: a small real lexical pull toward the broadcast text (about 1 point of Jaccard over the base) after principle-voiced consumes; modest, confounded by shared source. No judge, basin/repertoire measures not run (inferred, not verified semantics).
