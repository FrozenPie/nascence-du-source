## Answer in one paragraph

Yes, a random shock page reaches the self-model, and the route is not log adjacency: it is one shared recency feed,
`core/memory/store.py recent_world_material` (newest rows of origin study/reading/research, no age floor). The
subconscious T2 prompt (`nodes/subconscious_node.py` 1369, newest 6), the T4 world block (2724, newest 6, `WORLD_FED_T4`)
and the curiosity "Recently studied or read" block (`nodes/curiosity_node.py` `_select_study_material`, newest 9, 3 kept)
all read it, and a shock is by construction the newest row the moment it is stored. In the last 30 days a shock page was
in T2's newest-6 view during 53 of 202 written syntheses; 16 of those 55 exposures (29 percent) quote the page, against
6 of 269 (2 percent) for the pursuit-fetched pages in the same feed. It travels no further than that in 48 hours:
pursuits and standing questions are not touched, curiosity seeds never carry it, sleep replay strengthens it at about
twice the control rate. A by-id trace through `derived_from` is impossible (see below), so the by-id part is a
reconstruction of what the prompt saw, from fragment timestamps and the code.

## Method (what is by id and what is not)

* **By id, direct:** `memory_fragments.derived_from` names a shock fragment 0 times (0 of 70 shocks, 0 of 402 controls; 0
  of 322 / 1717 over the store). T2 writes `derived_from` = shadow-event ids (`subconscious_node.py` ~1514), not the
  fragments it was shown, so the lineage that exists for other rows does not exist for T2. `memory_links` touches no
  shock. `memory_edges`: shocks have none in 30 days (23 of 322 over the store, vs `answers->question` on nearly every
  pursuit page, 1708 of 1717). `replay_count` is a true by-id counter: used below.
* **By id, reconstructed:** the feed contents at each T2 / curiosity write time (fragment timestamps + the code's limits).
* **Lexical:** a later record "cites" a page if it holds the title string (tier 1), a distinctive title word (tier 2,
  background document frequency <= 0.2 percent of the 60k+ records), or two distinctive extract words (tier 3).
  Single words are weaker: two false positives found by reading the hits (pursuit 'arthur' = the King Arthur book; replay
  'bear' = a study note on bears), kept in the counts and named here.
* **Controls:** (a) 402 pursuit-fetched pages ("[Looked up: ... (pursuit: ...)]", same store call, same stored 0.8/0.8;
  current importance identical, median 0.585). There are NO "asked by him" rows in the whole store (J-3285's hand is not
  built), so "something he chose to look up" does not exist yet; the pursuit pages are chosen by a longest-word guess.
  (b) the 48 h BEFORE each page (an uninvited page should be absent there; a topical lookup is already in the stream).
  (c) a time-shift null: the same keywords counted in windows shifted by +-7/14 days.

## Can the downstream paths tell a shock from something chosen?

* **T2 and T4's world block: yes, in the text.** The line is `(research) ` + first 160 characters of the content, and the
  content starts `[Looked up: '<title>' (external shock - uninvited)]` (about 60 characters for a normal title). T2
  quotes it back ("an uninvited external shock that lands...", "the 'Dead Run' film's uninvited shock"). A pursuit page
  reads `(pursuit: ...)` in the same slot. No code branches on it; the model reads it.
* **Curiosity's study block: no.** `_select_study_material` keeps `content[content.find("]") + 1:]` and shows
  `(<subject>) <snippet>`, so the via phrase is cut off; a shock and a pursuit page look identical there ("(Evil Twin
  (film)) Evil Twin is a 2007 South Korean horror film..."). This is the one place the marker is missing, and it is where
  Evil Twin entered.
* **Retrieval prompts and the IMN seed:** already marked in master by J-3284 (`readable_label`, restart-gated).
* Not traced, not claimed: T4 self-authoring reads the same six rows; the sampled T4 log lines read "probationary: not admitted", and T4
  is on D-0664 hold.
