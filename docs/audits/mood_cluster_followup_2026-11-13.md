# Mood cluster follow-up: why opponent_process.b_after detached, and is ignition's lead only sampling cadence (J-3744)

Status: analysis, read-only on `C:/Nascence/data`, 2026-11-13. Follows J-3472 (`mood_cluster_defeater_read_2026-11-07.md`, F-4352 / F-4353) under D-0102. Script `scripts/analyze_j3744_mood_cluster_followup.py`; full machine output and tables in `docs/lab/J-3744_data/` (`mood_cluster_followup.json`, `mood_cluster_followup_tables.md`). Roll-null seed 42, 500 draws. Du was down while this ran; nothing was started and no model was called.

## Verdict

1. **`opponent_process.b_after` did not detach on its own: it detached when its feeder mix changed, on 2026-09-28.** The hourly gauge is the mean of `b_after` over the pair-activations in that hour. A first activation of a pair has `b_after` equal to a step that depends only on the valence sign (aversive 0.14, positive 0.07, neutral 0.03, times 0.35 without a progress signal), and the sign is a threshold of the valence at that moment, so first activations track valence at event time. A repeat activation has `b_after = min(ceiling, b_prev * exp(-gap/tau) + step)`, with tau of 2 to 6 hours: a slow integrator of the pair's own history, which carries almost no valence. The gauge is a blend of the two, so its coupling to valence is set by the blend.
2. **The blend moved because one feeder was added.** The `curiosity` source (calls through `core/memory_service.ask`) first appears in `co_recall_ledger.jsonl` at 2026-09-28 07:32Z, the same restart that picked up the D-0310 flags-off commit (7a167458, J-1587), which also turned ambient monologue recall into `monologue_shadow` rows. Before that, wandering plus monologue gave 80 percent of the rows with 57 percent first activations. From 10-07 on, curiosity is 53 percent of the rows with 12 percent first activations (46,537 activations over 5,520 distinct pairs, median 348 s between re-activations of a pair, so each pair sits near its ceiling): mean `b_after` for curiosity rows 0.495, mean `b_prev` 0.448, against 0.118 and 0.019 for wandering. The gauge's mean `b_prev` rose from 0.095 to 0.271.
3. **The numbers.** Hourly r of the gauge with `felt_preamble` valence: -0.59 before 09-28 (-0.62 in the F-0565 window, first-difference -0.49), -0.13 for 09-28 to 10-06, -0.32 from 10-07. Subsets: first activations alone -0.77, -0.76, -0.80 (never moved); wandering feeder alone -0.77 before, -0.80 after; the aversive share of rows -0.84 to -0.85 throughout. Removing the curiosity rows from the post-10-07 gauge gives -0.556 (n 504 h); removing wandering instead gives -0.294. Event time agrees: r of valence at the event with `b_after` is -0.64 to -0.72 for first activations in every period and -0.28 to -0.43 for repeats.
4. **The August coupling was not an artifact of a shared high-variance stretch.** It survives first differences (-0.49 for the gauge, -0.62 for first activations, in 08-21..09-02) and it has a mechanical reason: in August 63 percent of rows were first activations, so the gauge was mostly a sign-share meter. It was a feature of the then-feeder mix, not of the quantity the gauge is named for.
5. **Is there anything valence-like in the integrator part?** No. Hourly r of mean `b_prev` with valence is -0.22 before 09-28 and -0.03 to +0.18 after; in first differences -0.16 to +0.21.
6. **Ignition's lead over valence is not the two sampling cadences.** Both logs are per thought (median gap 43 s each); the last ignition row precedes a valence row by a median 12 s and the next follows by 30 s. Cadence effects live at tens of seconds. On a 60 s grid, detrended by a 3 h rolling mean, ignition leads valence at every lag from 1 to 20 min, with A(tau) (sign(C0) times the difference of the two lagged correlations) rising to +0.325 at 10 min (z +12.9 against the roll null) and +0.306 at 15 min (z +11.3), falling to +0.07 (z +2.2) at 30 min. Peak |C| is at +6 min (C = -0.43 at +5 min against -0.21 at -5 min). The lead is present before 09-04 (A +0.11 at 10 min, z +1.8; weaker because the series are nearly one quantity, C0 -0.80) and in the last 30 days (A +0.32, z +6.1). At the per-row level, among rows where the previous and next ignition rows are equally far (gap ratio 0.8 to 1.25, n 7,297, median gap 9 s each side), the partial correlation of valence with the previous ignition given the next is -0.107 and with the next given the previous is -0.001: a one-sided residual that equal sampling distance cannot produce.
7. **Reading of 6.** It is a lag of several minutes, matching F-4176's mechanism (the ignition term reads the dissonance reservoir per thought; the protoself updates the valence every 600 s), not a cognitive sequence. The 15-minute Levy area in J-3472 was measuring a real minutes-scale lead, not a bin artifact.

## What this does to D-0102 and the gauge registry

- The J-3472 recommendation (keep all six measured) stands, and the registry note it asked for can now be made precise for `opponent_process.b_after`: it is "valence sign at the moment of activation, plus a per-pair antagonist state that decays over hours", and the share of each depends on which loops are co-recalling. It can fall to near-zero valence coupling when one loop keeps re-activating the same pairs. A change in `b_after`'s coupling to valence is a statement about feeders, so read it together with the ledger source mix.
- If the aim is a valence-tracking member for the cluster, the part of the signal that does that job is the aversive share of rows (r -0.85 stable), not the mean `b_after`.

## Caveats

- Mechanism for items 1 and 2 is verified in code and data (the `opponent_process.py` step table, `memory_edges.log_co_recall`, the ledger join below). That the curiosity feeder is the whole cause is inferred from the counterfactual: removing its rows restores -0.556 but not -0.587, and the removal leaves its earlier activations inside the state that later non-curiosity rows decay from.
- Each opponent_process row was joined to its `co_recall_ledger` row by timestamp (within 2 s, earlier-or-equal) because the shadow log hardcodes `source="co_recall"`; 31 of 178,617 rows (0.02 percent) found no ledger row; the join is a nearest-timestamp match, not a key.
- I did not pin the exact commit that wires `curiosity` into `memory_service.ask`; the dated evidence is the first ledger row (09-28 07:32Z, same restart as the D-0310 flags-off), and `git log -S` on the loop string found nothing, so it is the J-1515/J-1542 series at best.
- The 10-03 effective date of J-1738 (monologue_shadow rows stop ticking opponent_process) is read from the daily source mix of the opponent_process rows (no `monologue_shadow` after 09-30 in them); the 09-28..10-02 window therefore holds phantom ticks (P2: 37 percent of its rows), which also depress the gauge's coupling there (-0.13).
- Part 2 uses last-observation-carried-forward with a 300 s staleness limit; Du was down for stretches, so paired points are 68k of the 189k grid minutes. The roll null shifts the valence grid by at least 6 h and detrends at 3 h, so it is not the sleep/wake-bout null of F-4150; J-3472 ran that one on 15 minute bins, and the two agree in sign and size of z.
- Nothing in `C:/Nascence/data` was written; only `docs/lab/J-3744_data/`, this file and the script were created.
