## 🔬 1. The Two-Axis Data Model

To prevent the system from getting buried under a wall of deficits (identity collapse), Du separates tension into two orthogonal axes:


1. **The Contradiction Axis (`source == 'critic_rejection'`):**  

   Fired when Carl/Du's internal Critic flags his own output as evasion or self-contradiction. Requires a minimum floor of **2 distinct rejections** to surface into identity loading.

2. **The Salience Axis (`source in ('unresolved_thought', 'unresolved_dream')`):**  

   Measures recurring thematic curiosity in 1536D space. Deliberately **NOT** surfaced as a self-contradiction; recurring on a theme is treated as genuine depth, not failure.
