## Reading

Six of ten are already in the works and correctly gated on the post-boot reads (3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9). The research
confirms their direction rather than changing it; the biggest confirmation is that dosed_coupling's "coordinates,
never prose" law and closure_law's "a met loop becomes a memory" are the two human patterns (meaning before words;
completion as a positive event) the literature supports most strongly.

What has NO plan is the representational medium itself: (1) a thought that is not a paragraph, (2) carrying the
thought as something other than its wording, (5) deciding which idle thoughts are written to memory at all. These
three are one idea, I-0095, and they are the part the three-model convergence actually added. They are also the only
part that would touch a base system (the idle thought producer and the episodic write path), so under the
erosion-of-foundations rule they can only be built as a second, shadow producer beside the live one, judged by a
decoupled judge, with the paragraph channel untouched.
