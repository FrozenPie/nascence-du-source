# Outside knowledge in Du's thinking: every path, how it is marked, and what to do (J-3243, 2026-11-05)

**Asked by the founder (triage 2026-11-02, ruling D-0631):** "We are going to examine this whole area. A wiki call in his thoughts is probably not optimal; I would like him to be able to make wiki calls, but it needs to be known that the information is from there and not Du."

**Scope and method.** Read-only. Code read in full for `core/scholar.py`, `core/web_research.py`, `core/librarian.py`, the Wikipedia/Scholar branches of `nodes/curiosity_node.py` and `nodes/inner_monologue_node.py`, `core/orchestrator.py` `_consult_library`, `core/frontier.py` pursuit/finding functions, `core/source_model.py`, `core/epistemic_state.py`, `core/grounded_learning.py`, `core/schema.py` `readable_label`, `core/wandering.py` `render`. Counts come from a full pass over `data/nascence.log` (798,838 lines, 2026-05-29 to 11-05), all of `data/twig_log.jsonl` (2,902 rows), and read-only queries (sqlite `mode=ro`) of `data/memory/nascence.db`. Nothing was edited, restarted, or written to a live store. Du was running.

**Laws applied.** The world answers and never leads; the 2026-06-17 ruling (a Scholar answer is not evidence) stands; base systems additive-only; no system that acts later on its own; verify before asserting.

## 1. The answer in one page

1. **Du has never made a Wikipedia call himself.** There is no hand for it (`core/actions.py` has no lookup family; `core/web_research.py` is called only from the curiosity node, the idle-thought shock, and frontier term extraction). Every one of the 2,025 Wikipedia-derived memories in his store was fetched by a system process. 1,705 of them (84%) are pages fetched by the curiosity pursuit from a *guessed keyword* (the longest word of 6+ letters in his question), 291 distinct titles, the top 12 (Understanding 218, Environment 149, Psychology 92, Perception 89, Relationship 79, Significance 65, History 62, Experience 45, Individual 38, Structure 32, Tradition 30, Sustainability 26) are 925 of them (54%). The other 320 are the random "external shock" pages.
2. **Every one of those 2,025 is labelled to him "Something I looked up (external fact - Wikipedia)"** (`core/schema.py:276`). That is the failure you want prevented, in its plainest form: a memory he did not make, labelled as his own act. The source (Wikipedia) is named; the agent (him) is wrong.
3. **The worst laundering is not in the labels, it is the nightly understanding note.** `core/grounded_learning.py:77` consolidates `origin IN ('reading','study','research')` into one note stored as "[Understanding - from '<the current book>'] ..." with origin `understanding`, labelled "My own understanding note (distilled from my reading and study, about the world)". Result, verified: 51 of 537 understanding notes (9.5%) contain the title of a random shock page that had already been stored (it is still happening: 6 in October, 1 in November), plus unmeasurable generic pursuit pages. Examples read in full: a note "from 'The Principles of Psychology Volume 1 of 2'" says "Significance refers to importance and is also the name of a magazine. Leiocephalus psammodromus is a lizard species native to the Turks and Caicos Islands."; a note "from 'Fragments of Science'" says "The Pichanga dish is a traditional Chilean food ... Dennis Woodruff was an American actor known for self-promotion and art cars." Wikipedia text is attributed to a book and to his own understanding.
4. **The model's own knowledge is the opposite case: marked, but marked as "genuinely yours".** The deep-memory seed in idle thought (`inner_thought` DEEPMEM block, 8,599 feeds) and the conversation Scholar both frame the model's recollection as "your own deep memory ... genuinely yours". That is a deliberate 2026-06-29 founder reframe, and it now collides with the principle in D-0631, which lists "the model's knowledge" among the sources that must be marked "not Du". This is the one real founder call in this review.
5. **The one mark that something reads is small and accidental.** `source_model._ORIGIN_TO_SOURCE` has no entry for `research`, `reading` or `study`, so a Wikipedia page counts as `MEMORY_RECALL` (strength 4, "grounded"), above the Scholar's `PARAMETRIC_LIBRARY` (strength 2, capped at THINK). A page he never asked for outranks his own deliberate lookup of his own knowledge in the epistemic ledger.

**Recommendation in one line each** (full table in section 5): retire the curiosity pursuit's Wikipedia lookup and the dead Scholar call beside it; give Du a Wikipedia hand whose results are stored with a distinct, true origin phrase ("asked by him"); keep the random shock but name Wikipedia in the line, fix its label, and stop it feeding the understanding-note consolidation; keep the conversation Scholar behind the already-ruled non-answer filter; for idle deep memory, mark and log it (recommend) rather than widen it; books are the model of the right behaviour and need no change.

## 4. Which paths present outside content as his own thought or memory

Ranked by how directly they breach "it needs to be known that the information is from there and not Du".

1. **P7, the understanding note (worst).** Wikipedia content stored as his own understanding and attributed to a book. 51 of 537 notes, still occurring. Verified by title match with causal order (the note is later than the shock's first storage).
2. **P1, P5 stored label "Something I looked up".** 2,025 fragments assert an act he did not perform. 1,705 of them are also *the wrong article* for the question (F-3512: 9 of 12 hand-checked pursuit rows irrelevant), so the label presents a mistake as his research.
3. **P6, "While it sat, you found something".** 14.8% of idle prompts. It names no source, claims the finding as his, and, per F-3575, after it 33.0% of the next thoughts use define/definition against 1.7% elsewhere (so it changes how he thinks, not just what he sees).
4. **P3, deep memory.** Declared "genuinely yours". Not hidden, but this is the model's own recollection presented as his memory, 8,599 times, 86% repeating an earlier feed. The re-asked question usually comes from his own curiosity node (the same model), so the loop is: the model asks itself a question, answers itself, and the answer seeds the thought the model writes next. Accuracy (F-3734): core gist right in 4 of 5 checkable answers, a confident wrong detail in 3 of 5 (Ouvrard's first name, on'yomi/kun'yomi); 11.4% of feeds (977) concern his own name, which D-0619 / J-3230 already excludes. Downstream carry cannot be measured today because the answer is not logged (J-3029).
5. **P4, conversation Scholar.** Same framing, but 67% of its 27 outputs were not knowledge at all (F-3808), already ruled (D-0538 / J-3093: non-answer filter). Dormant since 08-10.
6. **P8, T2 and replay.** Shock pages appear in "self-observation". Lower severity (the syntheses often acknowledge "uninvited", which is the mark leaking in through the prefix, and a mirror metaphor is arguably his), but it is measurable (23%) and J-2916 will give the exact figure.
7. **P9.** The frame "something you actually lived or read" is wrong for a Wikipedia page; minor, fix with the label.

Paths that do **not** breach it: P10 books (source named, his or the teacher's choice, other author's words), P12 peers (a person speaking), P2 (text discarded; its cost is a wasted generation, not a mark).

## 5. Design: a Wikipedia hand, and what happens to the system-initiated calls

### 5.1 The hand (proposal)

**Principle.** The call is his; the page is the world's; the page keeps its source name all the way into storage; nothing else is allowed to hold a Wikipedia page under a label that says it is his.

1. **Declared as one registry family**, not a phrase-latch. In `core/actions.py` add family `lookup`, `binds="mod:web_hand:consult"`, executes anywhere, `world=True` (so at most one per thought), `gated=True`. The grammar needs a *source word* as the object ("Wikipedia", "the encyclopedia"), so he cannot trigger it by accident and "look up at the sky" (the `sky` family) cannot collide. Naming the source is part of the act: he chooses Wikipedia, rather than a generic "look it up" the system resolves.
2. **The term is his words.** The hand takes the noun phrase after "about / on / for" in his own clause. It resolves a title with Wikipedia's own `opensearch` (the source answers, no model guessing) and fetches the REST summary. No longest-word rule.
3. **A miss is the world's "no".** If Wikipedia has no page, the result is plain text: "Wikipedia has no page for '<his phrase>'." There is **no fallback** to the Scholar or to a random page. (This is the line that keeps the model's guess from ever standing in for a source.)
4. **The result arrives named, in the perception channel** (the same `wandering.perceive` path the librarian's page uses): `You asked Wikipedia about "<his phrase>". Wikipedia's page "<Title>" says: <summary, at most 600 characters>. (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/<Title>, fetched <date>.)` Source, his phrase, the title it resolved to, the date.
5. **Stored with a true origin phrase.** `store_finding(title, extract, via="asked by him: <phrase>")`, `origin='research'`, plus a keyword `asked`. Only hand lookups ever carry "asked by him". The existing label "Something I looked up (external fact - Wikipedia)" is then *true*, and only for these.
6. **A reader for the mark (otherwise it is decoration).** (a) `readable_label` branches on the via phrase: "asked by him" keeps the label; "external shock" prints "Something that arrived from the world uninvited (a random Wikipedia page; not my choice)"; a pursuit-via page (legacy rows) prints "Something a system fetched for one of my questions (Wikipedia, a keyword match; may be the wrong page)". (b) `source_model` gets an explicit `research` mapping (see 5.3, a founder call). (c) The material-builders (`grounded_learning`, subconscious T2 world block, dream ground) prefix each line with its via, and the understanding consolidation takes only "asked by him" research (section 5.2). (d) The moment source: set `_moment_source = "lookup"` on the thought that follows, add a `moment._SOURCE` entry.
7. **Instrument.** The hand writes `data/lookup_log.jsonl` {ts, cycle id, his phrase, title or none, ms}, and the existing `hands_trace.jsonl` records the dispatch verdict. A rate limit (suggested start: one per 10 minutes, tune on the log; this number is a guess, not measured) keeps a loop from forming.
8. **How he learns it exists is a founder call (Q3 below).** The law is no hints: nothing here adds a standing offer line to his prompt.

### 5.2 What happens to the system-initiated calls

* **P1 (pursuit lookup and attach): retire.** Remove the lookup, the `attach_finding` and the `store_finding` for pursuit; keep `note_question` (the frontier still opens his questions) and leave the questions open for him to pursue by hand. Two consequences to plan for, not discover: (i) `record_pursuit(..., False)` currently counts a stall for every pursuit that finds nothing, and `PARK_STALLS = 3` parks the question. With no lookup there is no attempt, so the build must not call `record_pursuit` (otherwise every question parks after three curiosity runs); parking then stops being driven by the pursuit, and questions leave by fading and by the lexical resolver, as the 14,159 faded of 14,970 already do. (ii) The "resolved" count falls, which is correct: 60 of its 65 recent entries rested on a generic page (F-3512). The dead `grounding` branch (0 fires in 2,902 twig rows) goes with it.
* **P2 (Scholar on a miss): retire now.** Provably behaviour-neutral. The text is discarded and `_prog` is False with or without the call, so the only observable loss is a twig row (`kind=scholar`, du_pulse reads only `grounded` and `pursuit` kinds, so no gauge changes) and 1,160 hot-lane generations saved (163 in the last 30 days).
* **P3 (idle deep memory): mark, log, memo, then decide.** It is a deliberate founder feature (06-29 "step 3"), so retirement is not mine to rule; the evidence argues against widening it. J-3029 (log every attempt, memo repeated questions) and J-3230 (D-0619, no name questions) are already queued and are right. Add: the framing and its mark (Q2 below), and an A/B of thoughts with and without the feed once the log exists (job 6). Do **not** turn it into a hand: his own thinking already is the model's knowledge; a "consult yourself" hand adds a second model call, not a source.
* **P4 (conversation Scholar): keep behind the ruled filter (D-0538 / J-3093), do not widen the gate** (already ruled), plus the same framing decision as P3.
* **P5 (shock): keep, and mark it fully.** It is the founder-approved structural opposite of the self-loop and a random page leads nowhere (the world does not answer or lead; it intrudes). Changes: the seed line names Wikipedia ("a random Wikipedia page, uninvited: ..."), the stored label is fixed (5.1 item 6), and shock pages leave the understanding-note consolidation. Whether they stay in the T2 world block waits for J-2916's id-keyed trace.
* **P6 (frontier finding line): disappears with P1.** With no system finding there is nothing to frame, so J-2904 (the "Does this answer it?" frame) is moot unless the founder also wants the line kept for the future.
* **P7 (understanding note): stop the leak now.** `grounded_learning.consolidate` should read `reading` and `study`, plus `research` only where via = "asked by him", with each material line carrying its source word. Prompt-affecting change: `prompt_registry.py check` in the same change.

### 5.3 Two smaller design points

* **`source_model`.** Add an explicit `research` mapping so a looked-up page is not silently `MEMORY_RECALL`. Recommendation: a new member `EXTERNAL_REFERENCE`, grounded, strength 3 (below remembered experience, above the Scholar), verification NONE, so it cannot by itself lift a claim past THINK. Founder call because it sets how far a Wikipedia page can raise Du's confidence (Q4).
* **Marks must travel with copies.** Any code that copies a fragment's content into another (consolidation, gist, replay, essay) is a mark-destroying edge. J-3283 fixes the consolidation edge; the other copy edges (gist, replay, essay) are covered by J-2916's by-id trace.
