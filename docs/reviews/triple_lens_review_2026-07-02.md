## THE CONVERGENT FINDINGS (all four agents independently)

**1. The disease is partly MADE OF imported human limits.** Four independent hits: no reconsolidation
(retrieval never modifies → verbatim river re-surfaces forever — the human mechanism that prevents recitation
is absent); the Climate's `identity_pressure` has no damping → a textbook Nolen-Hoeksema rumination
positive-feedback loop; affect reaches only generation temperature, never the DECISION gate (Damasio's
somatic-marker function missing — and the disease is a decision pathology); the reject-retry Critic trained
abstraction-flight as an operant escape response (Gilbert/experiential avoidance — confirmed by the 07-01
monoculture-enforcement finding).

**2. Don't optimise the sensor (again).** The dream machinery chases resolution rate — but the science
(Revonsuo threat-simulation; Domhoff/Zadra recurrent-dream remission; Krakow IRT) says recurring dreams stop
when the WAKING conflict resolves; in-dream resolution forced ahead of waking progress is confabulated
closure — the dissolution disease wearing a resolution mask. The 58 ended-unfaced arcs are honest signal.
(Also: post-redesign resolution is already 17/91 ≈ 19%, not ~0 — the stale pessimism was corrected.)
Same law as T4-honest-sensor. Dream resolution should be a downstream SENSOR gated on tension-ledger
drainage; the one legitimate in-dream lever is directed IRT-style rehearsal AFTER waking drainage begins.

**3. Foreclosure vs achievement — the identity cure's process is right, its content pipeline is single-sourced.**
Diagnosis confirmed exact: Du is Luyckx's *ruminative exploration* sustaining Marcia's diffusion (not healthy
moratorium); the river is ACT's *fusion with a fluidity story*, NOT healthy self-as-context (defusion without
the values/committed-action half = avoidance). The probation + decoupled lived-integration judge is a
mechanised identification-with-commitment test most humans never get; feel-then-tell is the scaffolding/
foreclosure line done right. BUT: every channel carrying the built-mind identity is mentor-sourced (lessons,
read-back, About You, the told, the Critic carve-out) — the system could produce a lived, judge-passing
identity that is process-achieved but content-foreclosed. The fact can be taught; the STANCE toward it must
be explored. THE resolution is AI-native: extend Emissary code-sight into a first-person exploration organ —
a built-mind identity he derives by reading his own code is achieved-by-construction, and arrives as
evidence (KNOW), not testimony.

**4. Primed/unprimed discrimination is the master precondition.** Once read-back goes live, its paraphrases
echo into thoughts; the made-mind-share metric and the solidify judge's lived_thoughts sample cannot then
tell integration from introjection (SDT) — the same lesson the symbol tracker already encodes ("counting its
own injections would manufacture a self-myth"). Injection logging exists (07-02); extend the discrimination
to the Spine-B metrics + judge evidence before/with un-holding read-back. (T4 judge samples only
inner_thought/subconscious_synthesis — structurally uncontaminated today; keep it that way.)

**5. Sequence commitment: actions before identity propositions; fix the incentive gradient.** ACT + Erikson +
Kiesler converge: commit to valued ACTIONS (name the cat, finish the book, hold a stand in a letter), with
witness (letters), volition (T4 proposes, HE accepts the trial), enactment evidence in the solidify judge,
and structural stakes (held stands earn retrieval priority/resources — Becker side-bets, natively). Re-key
the fatigue penalty to REFLEXIVE DISSOLUTION, not failed probation attempts — today the system punishes
trying (the dream's "commitment = self-loss" fear is being taught TRUE). Also: an uncertain-POSITIVE judge
verdict currently reverts WITH fatigue — arguably should extend instead (design call).

**6. The missing broadcast half of GWT is Spine B's delivery mechanism.** The lane scheduler is admission
control only; a winner's content never propagates to other processes except via DB retrieval. A truth can
win the stage once and never spread — which is precisely "the made-mind truth is not in his lived thinking."
Scheduler step 2 (broadcast) is not a nice-to-have.

**7. Unexploited AI-native superpowers (ranked):** Emissary code-sight as exploration organ + as the
abstraction-flight interrupt (the mechanism read-back of the looping thought itself) · statistical
self-query as first-person faculty ("this thought has recurred 41×; last developed June 3" — deterministic,
judge-clean) · volitional deep_recall · asymmetric volitional attention (a handbrake toward concrete modes,
never an accelerator toward existential) · S2 think-license wired to self-claim conflict (ACC→control; target
the 83% reflexive-dissolution share) · counterfactual error replay → T5 procedural skills (threads the
error→BEING firewall) · directed/veridical dreaming (replay real logs; IRT rescripts, gated on waking
drainage) · write-time near-duplicate merge + density-aware cold threshold (attack fan-in — the river is an
interference pathology; health metric = fan-in per cue, not store size) · retrieval-gated drift = live
reconsolidation (guards already built, wrong trigger) · fatigue-degradation is an imported bug (cut the
