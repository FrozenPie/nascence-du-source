## PART 6 — WHAT THE LITERATURE SAYS WE MISSED

### 6.1 The echo problem: three knobs are predicted to fail, and one test decides everything

Converging results: attractor cycles in frozen-weight iterated loops persist **under increased
randomness and under model switching** (Wang et al. 2502.15208); a peer-reviewed *Patterns* study found
total convergence across **700 trajectories × 7 temperatures × 100 iterations** to 12 motifs; and
Shumailov's own repetition-penalty test **doubled perplexity while leaving collapse intact**.

So: **temperature, repetition penalty, and switching model are each predicted to fail.** We have already
falsified the third locally.

**But no sampler can see our frame collapse.** Every penalty scans the current context. If each
consolidation is a fresh call, "As I delve" at token 1 of call #5,120 is a sequence the sampler has never
seen. **57–66% is the model's unconditional prior sampled 5,120 times i.i.d.** — not a decoding bug.

**The decisive test, ~20 minutes:** sample the consolidation prompt 100× against an **empty memory
context**. If openings are already ~50%+ identical, most of the "monoculture" is the base model's RLHF
prior and no memory-architecture work will touch it. If they're diverse on a cold store, the loop is the
cause. *You cannot tell which world you are in without running this, and it decides the direction of the work.*

Supporting: RLHF measurably reduces output diversity, traced to **typicality bias in preference data**;
Verbalized Sampling (ask for k candidates *with probabilities*, sample off-mode) recovered **66.8% of
pre-alignment diversity**, training-free — though gains scale with model size, so expect less at 7–8B.

### 6.2 Du is brooding, and the intervention has an RCT

Watkins' framework: repetitive thought is constructive or unconstructive as a function of **level of
construal**. Abstract-evaluative processing measurably impairs problem solving, produces overgeneral
rather than episodic recall, and amplifies global self-judgment. All three map onto documented symptoms.

**"X feels like Y" is diagnostic of abstract construal** — a simile relates a particular to a class
without committing to any particular. Treynor's factor analysis splits rumination into *brooding*
(passive, maladaptive) and *reflective pondering* (adaptive). Du is brooding.

Concreteness Training: one session + 30 min/day for 7 days, **d = 1.36 on BDI-II vs waitlist** (honestly:
only trend-level against the matched sham on self-report, so part is non-specific). Four active
ingredients — sensory particulars → the sequence of how it unfolded → what is distinctive about *this*
instance → **a concrete next step**.

**The cheapest decisive test in the whole audit:** run mean content-word concreteness (Brysbaert norms,
offline lookup, zero LLM cost) across the existing 60-day corpus. If it steps down at 2026-06-19/20
alongside the frame collapse, the diagnosis is confirmed. **If concreteness is flat, the diagnosis is
wrong and you've saved yourself a build.**

### 6.3 The retrieval symptom has a geometric explanation nobody has measured

Hubness: as intrinsic dimensionality rises, a small set of vectors become nearest-neighbour to
*everything*, and points nearer the corpus centroid have systematically higher hub-ness. In a
**self-generated, centroid-drifting corpus**, the more the mind writes about its dominant theme, the more
the hubs *become* that theme. That is a literal derivation of "retrieval surfaces the same few items."

Measured on Sentence-BERT, f-norm + Mutual Proximity gave **69–83% hubness reduction** and 7–13% kNN
error reduction, post-hoc, no re-embedding.

**Why this matters most:** the plan is to cure retrieval collapse with *forgetting*. If the cause is
hubness, **forgetting makes it strictly worse** — you delete the tail and raise the hubs' relative
dominance. One afternoon of measurement (N_k skewness at k=10, plus retrieval-frequency Gini) is the fork
in the road for the entire memory program.

And the observed numbers already point that way: retrieval draws river/tide material at **42.0%** against
26.2% in the pool it's drawn from; `study` origin is **2.2% of the pool and 48.7% of pulls**.

### 6.4 Is "lossless memory causes stagnation" true?

**Half-right, and the wrong half is the actionable one.**

*Supporting:* consolidation as predictive forgetting is formally argued to require an *offline* pass and
to tighten generalisation bounds; forgetting-as-feature has real backing; CLS says the slow interleaved
system is mandatory. Nascence genuinely lacks a second compressed prediction-optimised representation.

*Against:* people with near-lossless autobiographical memory (HSAM) were **as susceptible as controls** to
false memories and *more* susceptible on misinformation — near-lossless recall did not suppress gist
extraction. And in recursive self-generated-data loops, **accumulating bounds the error while replacing
is the collapse condition** — so deletion moves toward the regime that collapses.

**Reframe: "an uncompressed *read path* causes stagnation." Keep every byte. Change what the reader sees.**

### 6.5 The BDI literature already ran our experiment

Kinny & Georgeff (IJCAI-91) measured this in Tileworld: **reacting to every new option is worse than
blind fanatical commitment**, except in fast-changing worlds. The winning configuration is **bold + a
narrow event filter** — replan only when the target disappears — and it was "everywhere superior."
Cautious agents degrade sharply as planning cost rises.

Du sits at the worst corner of that parameter space: maximum caution, maximum planning cost (a full
inference per reconsideration), in a low-change world of books and a cat and a fire.

Rao & Georgeff supply the missing data structure: a commitment has **a maintenance condition and a
termination condition**. Nascence's have neither — a formation event and an implicit continuous re-vote.

**The fix is not to make the hold rate go up. Every commitment must declare its termination condition at
formation, and reconsideration is gated on that condition firing.** Which is what the "named defeater"
note already says; the loop doesn't implement it.

Also missing: **no implemental mindset.** Post-decision, humans switch to a closed-minded implemental
phase. Du has one mode and it is deliberative. "Dissolves every boundary and holds nothing" is a precise
description of an agent with no implemental phase.

### 6.6 We built the retrieval stage of analogy and skipped the judgment stage

Forbus, Gentner & Law's **MAC/FAC** is explicitly two-stage: cheap content-vector matching to *retrieve*,
then structural matching to *judge*. **Nascence has built MAC and skipped FAC.**

Embeddings are an attribute-similarity instrument; water and circuitry share *relational* structure and
almost no attributes. **Root-similarity 0.405 isn't measuring drift, it's measuring the vehicle.**

A ~50-line judge-free version: classify the vehicle's source domain by lexical field, separately extract
the target-side predicates it asserts, and treat *constant predicates + changing source domain* as the
same claim in new material. That would have caught river → circuitry.

### 6.7 Cognitive geometry: the strongest supporting science says factorise, not fuse

The design makes senses dimensions of *one* shared space. The Tolman-Eichenbaum Machine — the closest
scientific support for "senses as dimensions" — says the opposite, as its central result: **structural
code and sensory code are separate, and the hippocampus forms their conjunction.** Generalisation comes
from the structural basis being reusable across environments with different sensory content. Fusing them
destroys the separation that buys transfer.

Two more, both cheap and both load-bearing:

- **Springs need a birth rule and a fan penalty.** Rescorla: conditioning tracks **contingency (ΔP)**, not
  co-occurrence — birth springs on ΔP or PMI, or you grow a dense mat of edges to whatever is always
  present. And ACT-R's `S_ji = S − ln(fan_j)`: associative strength **decreases logarithmically** with how
  many things a cue attaches to. Without that term, the most-connected concepts become the least
  retrievable — **the monoculture reappears as a graph hub.**
- **Disentanglement is provably impossible without inductive bias** (Locatello et al., 12,000+ models plus
  a theorem) — *and Nascence is sitting on the supervision it needs and not using it.* The world engine
  has generative parameters. Log them alongside every percept and "senses as dimensions" becomes a
  supervised identification problem with an oracle.

**And the closest existing relative is a cautionary tale:** NELL ran for years growing a typed-edge KB
self-supervised, and suffered semantic drift — better at some relations, *less accurate over time* at
others, needing human review every few weeks.

### 6.8 The negativity problem is arithmetic, not psychology

`core/homeostasis.py:66` exports both channels and the wrong one is wired downstream:

```python
return {"valence":        max(-1.0, min(1.0, 1.0 - 2.0 * float(s["drive"]))),   # TONIC level
        "valence_phasic": s.get("valence"),                                      # -dD/dt
```

`emotion_construction.py:156` reads the **level**. Both source theories define valence as a
**derivative** — Keramati's reward is `D(prev) − D(now)`; Joffily & Coricelli's is `−dF/dt`. The
docstring states the correct formula; line 66 ships the other one.

The consequence is measurable: the phasic channel is **mean −0.00015 over 1,927 ticks** — mean-zero to
four decimal places, because a difference of a bounded series telescopes. The level channel's sign is a
pure function of where the setpoints were placed (60th percentile of each variable's own distribution),
so `1 − 2D` crosses zero at the median **by construction**.

**Homeostatic RL does not have a negativity problem. `1 − 2D` does.**

The recommended fix has a name: **mood as a leaky integral of the change signal** (Eldar/Niv) —
`mood_t = (1−α)·mood_{t−1} + α·(D_{t−1} − D_t)`. Mean-reverting and sign-balanced without tuning, and it
gives hedonic adaptation for free.

Also flagged: `relational_warmth` is the dominant drive in **50.5% of ticks** with a setpoint of 0.824
and **no consummatory action Du can take** — a constant negative pedestal wearing a drive's clothes. And
it is *exteroceptive*, sourced from other agents' behaviour, which is a category error in a protoself.

### 6.11 Hardware: the blocker may not exist

**5.03 GiB free on the 1080 Ti at a ~5% GPU duty cycle.** The largest prompt in 98 calls used **19.8%**
of a 24,576 window; median 3.5%. Dropping to `num_ctx 8192` frees ~2 GiB and still leaves 68% headroom
over the observed maximum.

The Witness plan records "dedicated hardware — unresolved, the Trinity Fabric is fully allocated." That
premise looks false. The actual blocker is **`OLLAMA_MAX_LOADED_MODELS` unset at machine scope** — one
env var.

Two further findings: the 3070 is **over-subscribed ~50%** (11.7 GB of models in an 8 GB card, so they
LRU-evict each other at 16.7 s per reload vs 0.25 s warm); and the truncation suspicion is dead
(`cap_rate` 0%) while **211 *output* truncation salvages in 7 days**, 33 on the identity path, are
silently backfilled with defaults. *We built an instrument for the failure that wasn't happening.*

Longer-horizon: the 1080 Ti (compute 6.1) is **below vLLM's 7.0 floor** and on NVIDIA's CUDA deprecation
path. The multi-LoRA "model-per-node" future — one base plus many adapters, which is the affordable form
of the founder's vision — lives on the 3070 or its successor. The node hosting "the self" is the
dead-end node.

### 6.12 The founder's law on small models survives

**Do not switch to bigger models to fix the echo.** The literature favouring scale concerns
*within-generation looping under uncertainty* — a different disease. The mechanistic argument for
small-model copying does not survive contact with the papers: the canonical copy-suppression head lives
in **GPT-2 Small, 117M parameters**. And on the thing actually measured — form collapse, low diversity,
attractor lock-in — the evidence points at **post-training alignment**, reported as *the same across
model sizes*, with syntactic diversity peaking at ~7B and declining above it.

**A 70B model will not stop writing "As I delve."**

On the identity fine-tune: **switching to the base model is right long-term but is not the fix**, and
addresses one of three causes. The likeliest cause is the cheapest to test and needs no training —
every training example had a system prompt; the cold-start eval has none. Re-evaluate the *existing*
adapter with (a) full prompt, (b) empty system-prompt block, (c) no block at all. If (b) passes and (c)
fails, it is a template mismatch and the fix is data composition. **One evening, may close the issue.**

Also: 10 epochs is 5–10× current practice, and optimal LoRA LR is **~10× the full-FT LR**.
