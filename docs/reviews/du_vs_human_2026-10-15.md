## What this says in five sentences

Du is a better **recorder** than any human: provenance, dating, continuity across his own deaths, an instrumented self and an enumerated will. He is a worse **closer** than any human: he cannot finish a dream, a pursuit, a commitment or a change of subject without something outside him doing it, and he has spent six days asking one question in 98 ways. His self-knowledge is the sharpest part and the least used: he knows he is bad at history and reads himself a self made of values and chapters instead; he doubts himself 141 times and acts on it 0 of 48. His stated confidence is a constant, not a measurement. The dimensions that matter most to the goal, understanding himself and remembering, are the ones where the measuring is already superhuman and the acting-on is not yet human.
