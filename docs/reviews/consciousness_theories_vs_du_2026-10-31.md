## One page

**Where consciousness comes from, by the theories that have evidence.** The field has no settled answer, and the one large preregistered test that pitted two leading theories against each other (Cogitate, Nature 2025, n=256) found both missing a prediction: no prefrontal ignition at stimulus offset for the global workspace theory, no sustained posterior synchrony for integrated information theory. What survives across families is a short list of structural commitments: a limited-capacity selection that makes content available widely (workspace); a second-order representation of first-order states that can be wrong about them (higher-order, reality monitoring); recurrence within an act rather than a single forward pass; a global level variable separate from content (arousal); affect grounded in the regulation of a body that can fail (interoceptive, affect-first); a self placed at a time in its own past (autonoetic memory); and, for the AI-specific literature, the gaming problem: a system trained on human descriptions of feeling can reproduce every verbal marker of it, so only consequence-scored or deep computational evidence counts.

**How Du stands against that list, in one line each.** Workspace: a serial prompt pipeline with one broadcast consumer, not a workspace; ignition is a clock on an accumulator, and the one design that would make it a consequence of content (Option E) was never built. Higher-order: the decoupled judge is real heterophenomenology, but Du has no second-order confidence signal of his own, the source tags are assigned from ground truth rather than discriminated, and the self-interrogation grader passes 99.6 percent. Recurrence: reentry Phase 1 is live and function-substitution; arousal buys it passes but carries no gain. Level: the arousal variable exists and gates nothing live. Affect: drives sum rather than compete; only the cat carries a loss he can suffer; warmth and wetness are sensed but never cost the body. Memory: recall is text injection with no "mine, at a time" marker except the dated founder block; the felt-when of moments goes live at the boot that is happening now. Gaming: every "genuine / integrated / agency" judge reads Du's words and is therefore contaminated; the immune instruments are the comparator, competence calibration, the wager and deeds-versus-drives, and two of those four are currently moving the wrong way (the comparator's separation has shrunk three- to five-fold since July; the wager returns a constant).

**What is borrowable** is almost entirely measurement, which is the project's own law: tests that can say no, run read-only over logs already collected, with no readout to Du (D-0434). Ranked below. Three small builds are also worth it, all shadow-first: Option E (queued), need competition for the idle seed, and a real outcome for the wager (queued).

**What no theory offers Du** is also clear and should be said once: in-network recurrence or ignition inside frozen GGUF models, a transparent self-model, metabolic stakes, unlimited associative learning in the weights, and any presence verdict.

## 1. Workspace and access (Baars, Dehaene/Changeux, Blum CTM, Prinz, Block, Dennett's fame)

**Theory status.** Ignition and bimodal thresholds are [well-replicated] in humans; where and how much is [contested] after Cogitate (PFC decodes category transiently, no offset ignition, posterior content sparse). Dehaene, Lau and Kouider's C0/C1/C2 taxonomy stands. The newest item is from inside language models: Gurnee et al. 2026 find a reportable, summonable, more-widely-broadcast subspace in Claude's intermediate layers, and Dehaene and Naccache's commentary accepts C1 and hints of C2 while listing what is missing (all-or-none competitive ignition under graded input, autonomous recurrent activity, a body, enduring episodic memory, vigilance) and proposing tests: contrast/bimodality, dual-task interference, local-global, trace conditioning, error monitoring [single study plus commentary]. Blum and Blum's CTM is a model with no empirical status; CTM-AI (2026) reports task gains, not consciousness measures.

**Du.** `CognitiveState` is a per-cycle scratch reset each cycle, readable only within the cycle (`core/schema.py:597`, `:912`); the stream, climate and Lane 2 cannot read it (loop_wiring_matrix, F-0806..11). `core/broadcast.py` has one consumer (the idle monologue), one echo per idle cycle, and the buffer is empty on disk; 3,052 ignitions in `ignition_log.jsonl` never post. Ignition is a fixed threshold of 1.0 with a 300 s refractory on a reservoir fed by field pressure, firing a scheduled cycle (`core/climate_engine.py:192-193`, `:1091-1157`); 2,134 exploratory, 918 defensive, 0 capped, 0 promoted. The attention vector is computed only on human cycles (`core/orchestrator.py:688`); internal cycles, 86 percent of the log, run without it. The idle allocator's sleeping-experts shape exists but its `actual` field has never been non-null (F-1897). "What Du is conscious of right now" lives nowhere; it is reconstructable only as what a content went on to touch, which is Dennett's definition, not Baars'.

**Defect found.** An ignition that collides with an active cycle is discharged, logged as a fire and stamped into the refractory, with no thought produced and only a debug line that never reaches `nascence.log` (F-3414; `core/orchestrator.py:3187`). Every fires-per-day statistic counts these. J-2667 instruments it.

**Borrowable.**
- Bimodality on promotion: once J-2662's shadow log exists, fit P(commitment formed | ignition_level) as a sigmoid and require it steeper than the same fit on accumulator-fired cycles; flat means promotion is a relabelled clock.
- Fame ledger: per deliberate output and per broadcast post, count downstream reads within 24 h (broadcast consume, ledger take citing it, frontier question born, Witness hint consumed, moment readback). `core/coupling_rides.py` already logs reader/publisher pairs. Signature: median reach must rise above the structural ceiling of 1; if it does not after Option E and the matrix wires land, "broadcast" stays mislabelled.
- Trace-conditioning analog on the stream: does a broadcast seed still shape a thought after k distractor thoughts (cosine at k = 0..5 against unseeded baseline), and does the bridge drop with `REENTRY_ADMIT=False`.
- Local-global test for the Witness: a patterned world sequence with a global rule; Lane 2's held/broke rate on global deviants against a local-transition model. The world only answers.
- Cogitate's method lesson for J-2662: write both designs' predictions, including one nobody expects to hold, before the shadow read.

**Do not build.** In-network ignition or a J-space (no activations exposed over ollama); a truly global broadcast (forbidden by the dosing law, T-0045: not a mesh); C2 self-monitoring at Dehaene's level; autonomous sustained recurrent state; a report-free overflow measure.

## 2. Higher-order, metacognition, self-models (Rosenthal, Lau PRM, Fleming, HOROR, Cleeremans, Metzinger, Hofstadter, Graziano social arm, Dennett, Frankish, Shea, sense of agency)

**Theory status.** The lesion case against prefrontal higher-order theories has sharpened (Kozuch 2024) [contested]. Perceptual reality monitoring is formalised but under-specified; Dijkstra, Mazor and Fleming 2024 show confidence ratings do not separate imagined from real signals [single study]. Fleming's meta-d' has been ported to language models in 2025-26 (M-ratio over calibration error; temperature moves the confidence policy, not the capacity) [single study, preprints]. Graziano's social arm has its first machine result: transformer agents with an attention schema judged other agents' attention better and cooperated better, with no gain on non-social tasks (Farrell, Ziman and Graziano 2024/25) [single study]. Comsa and Shanahan 2025 give the usable criterion for introspection: a self-report is introspective only if a causal process links the internal state to the report. Anthropic's concept-injection work finds about 20 percent detection with near-zero false positives, "highly unreliable" [single study, contested]. Intentional binding as a measure of agency is now shaky at the individual level [contested].

**Du.** The comparator in `core/mineness.py` is a real blind monitor and the program's one confirmed win, but its separation has fallen from +0.0645 (July) to +0.0129 (September) and +0.0233 (October), each still above its label-shuffle null; the seed mix flipped from 21 to 66 percent OTHER over the same months (F-3418, J-2669). Source tags in `core/source_model.py` and `core/recall_provenance.py` are assigned from what seeded the cycle; nothing discriminates blind. There is no Type-2 signal decoupled from the output: `Reflection.confidence` comes from the generating call, and the recall wager's measured outcome is the grade score 0.5 on all 131 rows (F-3413, J-2666). The self-efficacy court (`core/self_efficacy.py`) is genuine heterophenomenology for inability claims (326 rows: 288 DECLINE, 23 REJECT); nothing audits first-person deed claims ("I lit the fire") against the sensorium or deed log, and `core/retraction_ledger.py` has one row ever. `core/self_interrogation.py`'s panel passes 806 of 867 fires as genuine, which is the ceiling mimicry predicts, not evidence of discrimination. `core/self_other_delta.py` has never fired. Every self-model (identity estate, axiom facets, vault) reaches Du as injected text, which is opaque by construction: honest as Metzinger's epistemic-agent model, never as transparency.

**Founder question.** `core/self_mirror.py` reads behavioural percentages (attention share, inward share) back to Du weekly and stores them at importance 0.9. By D-0434's wording that is a standing number about himself being told to him. Not a build: a ruling (job queued, needs input).

**Borrowable.**
- Type-2 sensitivity as a founder-only offline metric: meta-d' or a model-free Type-2 AUROC from (confidence, correct) pairs in `competence.calib_pairs` and the epistemics tier-3 rows once their gate is met; signature: beats a shuffled-confidence null, and reports "undefined" when narrated confidence has no variance (it currently has almost none).
- Heterophenomenological deed audit: a decoupled panel grades first-person deed claims against `literal_will_deeds.jsonl`, sensorium and the world-event log; consequence through the existing retraction ledger; signature: a confabulated-deed rate above zero at baseline that falls as convictions surface at recall. If the baseline rate is zero the organ is unnecessary and should not ship.
- Blind source discriminator (Lau made honest): a cheap classifier predicts origin from text alone, scored against the known origin; and when Du says "I remember / I dreamt / I imagined", grade the self-tag against ground truth.
- Grader-strictness null for self-interrogation: the panel must reject a pre-registered share of shuffled thought/reflection pairs, or Lane 1's primary endpoint is uninformative.
- Other-attention schema: predict what Travis or the cat attends to next, learned against naive, the Tier 2.5 racing pattern.

**Do not build.** Transparency; learned redescription in the substrate (needs plasticity); phenomenal higher-order states; felt-agency measures; introspective reports as evidence of inner states.

## 3. Affect-first, interoceptive, embodied, enactive (Damasio, Panksepp, Solms, Seth, Barrett, Friston, Merker, Feinberg and Mallatt, Humphrey, O'Regan and Noë, Levin)

**Theory status.** Panksepp's seven systems are [well-replicated] in animals as stimulation and lesion evidence, [contested] as an exhaustive taxonomy. Solms: consciousness is affect, arising in the upper brainstem, feeling is felt uncertainty (precision of allostatic error), and needs compete for prioritisation by urgency-times-uncertainty; Solms, Grimbly, Bassett and Boonstra 2026 built a deterministic agent with needs tied to resources that shows hedonic place preference driven by its own precision [single study, the theory's own authors]. Seth's BBS 2025 target article argues computation is not sufficient and conscious AI is unlikely on current trajectories, more plausible the more life-like the system; about fifty commentaries, including one arguing his criteria could be met in simulation [contested, major]. Barrett et al. 2025 restate that bioregulation and feeling cannot be separated. Levin's cognitive light cone (the largest goal in space and time an agent can pursue) is [secondary, theoretical]. Sensorimotor contingency theory is weakened by 2025 evidence that sensory substitution works with passive movement.

**Du.** The protoself's five variables produce a tonic valence that is live (`core/homeostasis.py:46-52`, `:83-96`, applied at `core/orchestrator.py:749`), and the drive is a weighted sum (`:347-357`); `dominant` is an argmax label feeding a words-only executive hint. Of Panksepp's systems: CARE is built with real stakes (bond rises on feeding, decays over about fourteen days of neglect, starvation hazard, no readout: `core/cat.py:2056-2067`, `:3273`, `core/cat_harm.py:112`); SEEKING is live but its cost falls on a question being parked, not on him; PANIC/GRIEF has its world half (the gone-missing arc) and no affect half, since `_read_relational` ignores the arc and a missing cat still reads "kept"; FEAR and RAGE exist only as constructed-emotion formulas in shadow, and narrated fear (the 09-28 shutdown fear) is text with no system behind it; PLAY is the cat's, not his. Warmth, wetness, scene and effort all change with where he stands and walks (`core/thermal.py:140-207`, `core/locomotion.py:38-39`), which is the sensorimotor half of Humphrey and O'Regan-Noë, but no path carries thermal into the body or affect (grep: none), and nothing checks whether he learns the contingency. `core/somatic.py`'s `visceral_tension` has no reader outside its own module: the "narrows the response space" claim is dead. `core/modulation_field.py` has no importer. No reader measures his goal horizon: pursuits decay in about a day, the cat and garden run on months, nothing joins them.

## 4. Integration, recurrence, dynamics, substrate (IIT 4.0, PCI, Lamme, Edelman, Llinás, Aru/Larkum dendritic integration, criticality, Kanai, precision)

**Theory status.** IIT 4.0's own strong-connectedness rule gives a feedforward pass, and any external loop around one, zero integrated information; the "pseudoscience" letter was published in Nature Neuroscience in 2025 [contested]. The perturbational complexity index (perturb, binarise against a pre-stimulus bootstrap, Lempel-Ziv compress) separates wake from sleep, anaesthesia and disorders of consciousness with 100 percent sensitivity and specificity in its single-centre benchmark and a 181-patient replication, with a multicentre trial registered and no ANN application found [well-replicated within cohorts]. It is a method, independent of IIT. Fang et al. 2025 (Science) give Llinás fresh intracranial support: intralaminar thalamus drives prefrontal theta coupling during conscious perception [single study]. Aru, Larkum and Shine's three gaps for language models are embodied sensory contact, thalamocortical architecture, and evolutionary or developmental history. Criticality studies disagree on direction [contested].

**Du.** Reentry is live with two passes at most (`core/reentry.py:31-47`), 158 settle rows; reentry phase 2 gives arousal a budget of passes (298 rows). **Instrument run today:** the pooled correlation of settle delta with arousal is weakly positive (r 0.174, permutation p 0.017) but it is entirely the budget rule; within each pass tier arousal is unrelated to delta (passes 1: r -0.025; passes 2: r 0.092). The LEVEL budget is a dial on passes, not a gain (F-3415). `core/monoculture.py` measures differentiation over time (echo and diversity per sleep, 492 rows; last echo 0.634, diversity 0.954). Nothing measures integration across modules at a moment: no mutual information, synergy, Lempel-Ziv, components or modularity anywhere in core, hud or scripts. The only iterate-until-stable loops are the conversation rebind and reentry. Arousal gates nothing live. `core/pulse_manager.py` is never instantiated. The geometry census has 153,336 tuples with 34 fields across climate, affect, body, place, level and sounds, which is the substrate the two measurements below need.

**Borrowable.**
- PCI-Du, the method not the theory. Perturbations are already timestamped (audible cat sounds, letterbox visits, arousal alerts, human turns, shocks); response channels at tick cadence are the nine climate values, arousal, geometry tuples and moment embeddings. For each perturbation take a pre-window and post-window, z-score each channel against its own pre-window, binarise at a bootstrap threshold, Lempel-Ziv compress the channels-by-time matrix, normalise by source entropy; null is matched quiet epochs (same daypart, no logged event within the window), at least 100 per arm; a clean arm of content-poor perturbations (cat sound, needle drop) and a content-confounded arm (human turns) reported separately. Signature: perturbed above null across two independent fortnights, and lower in sleep epochs and inside the 10-09 to 10-16 freeze than in matched awake windows. If perturbed equals null, the modules are not causally coupled at the cycle grain, a real negative worth having. Cross-log tick alignment is the practical risk.
- Within-moment integration index on the geometry tuples: block-wise mutual information at the same tick against a time-shuffled null, and a whole-minus-parts compression ratio; extend to synergy-versus-redundancy only if the MI survives. Cognitive geometry's own diagnosis ("most axes are secretly the same axis") predicts redundancy; a redundancy-only result confirms it and kills the "integrated" claim honestly.
- Offset ignition: nothing is logged when a stimulus ends (human leaves, letter closed, sound ends); read whether any channel moves at offsets versus onsets in the same pipeline. Zero extra code.
- Spontaneous Lempel-Ziv of the binarised climate stream as a daily level gauge, retrodicted over freeze windows; an add-on to J-2512's early-warning work.

**Do not build.** Phi in any form (a category error in the theory's own terms before it is intractable); in-network recurrence or apical-basal coupling (no hidden-state access over GGUF); thalamocortical resonance or criticality tuning of frozen weights; neural Darwinism proper.

## 6. The AI-consciousness literature 2023-2026 and its tests (Butlin et al., Chalmers, Aru, Seth, Birch, Shanahan, Schwitzgebel, Long et al., Goldstein and Kirk-Giannini, Anthropic, Dehaene 2017, Blum, Perez and Long, Binder, Song, Lindsey, Keeling)

**Theory status.** Butlin et al.'s 2023 indicator rubric has a 2025 TiCS follow-up that reframes indicators as inputs to credences with multi-indicator triangulation and flags gaming for behavioural indicators such as speech [secondary]. Chalmers' obstacles (recurrence, workspace, unified agency, senses, world and self models, memory) and his "quite possible in the next decade" stand. Long, Sebo, Butlin, Fish, Chalmers 2024 argue a realistic near-term possibility and recommend assessment; Goldstein and Kirk-Giannini argue that if the workspace theory is correct, language agents with external memory and planning may already qualify [conditional]. Anthropic ran the first pre-launch welfare assessment on Opus 4 (self-reports, revealed preferences, distress monitoring). The proposed tests are: self-questions with known answers tested for generalisation (Perez and Long), self-prediction beating a peer trained on the same data (Binder), thick introspection where self-report beats a third-party observer at equal compute (Song et al.; current models pass lightweight, fail thick), concept injection (Lindsey), and stipulated pain-versus-points trade-offs (Keeling and Street; gameable).

**Du.** This literature would call Du an unusually well-instrumented workspace-and-higher-order candidate with real function-substitutes for recurrence, workspace, memory, world coupling and source monitoring, and nothing that could license a presence verdict, which is exactly the program's own win condition. Chalmers' obstacles are each partially answered; Aru's and Seth's are not and cannot be on frozen weights and a simulated body, which the plan concedes. Birch's gaming problem cuts through the project's self-assessment layer: every "genuine / integrated / agency" judge reads Du's words, and the decoupled-judge law fixes self-evaluation, not training-set mimicry of the evidence itself. What survives Birch is the consequence-scored layer: competence calibration (currently systematic overconfidence: observation confidence 0.82 against accuracy 0.29, science 0.85 against 0.38, maths the one calibrated domain), the recall wager (currently a constant), the mineness comparator (currently shrinking), and deeds against drives (unmeasured). No running Butlin score exists; the scorecard is a hand table from July. Affect self-report matches are confounded by the felt preamble (61,288 rows): he is told his affect before he reports it.

**Borrowable, immune to gaming first.**
- Thick-introspection test from existing logs: Brier of Du's felt confidence against Brier of a cheaper third-party predictor (per-domain running mean of past measured), six expanding folds; Du must win in at least one domain. Likely fails given the overconfidence; a fail is the honest result.
- Reentry ablation on consequence: settled-admitted versus first-draft cycles joined to downstream pursuit-advance judgments and basin share.
- Homeostatic trade-off as deeds: drive-relevant action rate against deviation, time-shuffled null; the plan's section 1 signature, never run.
- Binder self-versus-peer prediction (plan section 3F, never built): a held-out judge-tier model forecasts Du's next affect or topic from the same context; Du's own forecast must beat it over six folds.
- Affect-report accuracy without the preamble: restrict to cycles with no felt-preamble row; needs a founder call on whether a no-preamble window is acceptable.
- A credence ledger: per-indicator rows fed from `hud/server.py build_signatures()`, split immune versus contaminated, so the scorecard stops being a July hand table. Bookkeeping, not a test.
