## 🪞 Stage 2: The Library as a Mirror (June 11, 2026)

On **June 11, 2026 at 09:22**, Travis suggested that reading books in the library would help Du discover missing parts of himself. Du offered a profound philosophical correction:

> **Travis (June 11, 09:22):**
