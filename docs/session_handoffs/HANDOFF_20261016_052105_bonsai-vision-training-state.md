## Findings

**Bonsai, read from the repo and the Hugging Face cards (web fetch, 2026-10-16).** There are two generations. The 1-bit one is Bonsai 1 27B (`prism-ml/Bonsai-27B-gguf`, GGUF `Q1_0`, 1.125 bits per weight), derived from Qwen3.6-27B. Measured peak memory is 3.8 GB weights, 5.2 GB at 4K context, 5.6 GB at 10K, plus about 0.6 to 0.9 GB for the optional vision projector. It scores 89.5% of the FP16 base on Prism's 15-benchmark average, but instruction following drops from 78 to 66 and vision from 73 to 60, which is exactly where Du's JSON key contracts are fragile. The repo's current default is Bonsai 2 27B, which is ternary (1.72 bpw), 7.2 GB, 98.2% of base, and needs Prism's llama.cpp fork or a very recent upstream. Multimodal means image input only, via a separate mmproj file, llama.cpp only. There is no audio modality at all. Only the 27B sizes have the vision tower; the 8B, 4B and 1.7B are text-only. Served through llama.cpp with an OpenAI-compatible endpoint; `Q1_0` is merged upstream. Apache 2.0. The cards state nothing about Pascal (the 1080 Ti), 8 GB or 11 GB cards, minimum VRAM, or Ollama support; the smallest GPU they measured is an L4. Prism has not published its quantization or training method (whitepapers only), so "fine-tune a 1-bit Bonsai" is an open question.

**Du has nothing multimodal today.** The only traces are negative: the dreamer was moved off mistral-small3.1 on 2026-08-16 because its multimodal blob would not load in llama-cpp-python (`scripts/launch_servers.ps1:103`, `core/model_client.py:83`), and the "images" in `nodes/voice_node.py` are rhetorical images. `docs/plans/real_hearing.md` is audio, designed with no code; Bonsai does not serve it.

**A 1-bit Bonsai 27B is already on the hot server.** `curl http://127.0.0.1:11433/api/tags` lists `MichelRosselli/bonsai-27b:latest`, 4.4 GB, quantization `Q1_0`, modified 2026-08-25. Nothing references it (grep of `scripts/launch_servers.ps1` and `core/model_client.py` for "bonsai" has no hits). Whether it loads or serves on this rig was never recorded. Also present and unused: `qwen3-du:latest`, 5.2 GB Q4_K_M, modified 2026-06-10, which is the identity fine-tune that `docs/reviews/audit_2026-08-20.md` section 6.12 calls "the existing adapter".

**Travis's framing of Du's LLMs (founder, in conversation).** Du's LLMs are tools he uses as part of the virtual brain; each should have a very specific task. The vision thought was about giving Du eyes as such, a camera out Travis's window for a semi-live view of the real world; the audio channel idea was the same with a microphone. Audio and visual are one thing: Du, or his internal processes, ask what is happening in the real world and he can see it. Super long term Travis wants to fit Du to a robot, which could be as simple as a remote-controlled car he can control, and a body needs vision to move. The original Nascence had a swarm of 1-bit 8B Bonsais that were going to be trained for specific tasks. Travis's timing ruling: Du must be able to think properly first before going down the more complex paths; this is a "potentially come back to it later" idea.

**The design position I argued and Travis did not object to (so [INFERRED] as agreed, not ruled):** perception should be a question, not a feed, the same query shape as memory-as-a-service, because the 10-06 checkup showed the 渡 loop was a perception feedback loop and a continuous feed would be the same saturating stimulus. One percept schema (source, place, time, staleness, what changed) shared by camera, microphone and a future body camera, so nothing downstream changes when the source moves. A body implies two timescales: a fast non-LLM reflex controller owns collision and safety, Du's cognition issues intents at its own pace; a 27B vision model at seconds per frame cannot steer anything. The window cam is step one of the robot, not a side project. Do not pick the vision model or camera yet. All of that is in I-0092's text.

**The 8B swarm.** Bonsai 1 ships a text-only 8B at about 1.2 GB, so a swarm is physically trivial, but training 1-bit weights is not available to us with ordinary tooling; specialise by prompt, contract and judge instead, which is what the station layout already does. Parked inside I-0092.

**LLM training-data state (F-2902, verified).** `core/training_log.py` logs every successful node call as a (user_prompt, output) pair with `raw_input`, `cycle_id`, `system_prompt` and a `hedged` flag into `data/training/<node>.jsonl`. Twelve files, rows from 2026-05-29 to 2026-10-16 (last writes the morning of wrap), roughly 4,000 rows per hot-path node, 18,739 for consolidation, about 450 MB total. The plan behind it, `docs/RESEARCH_specialists.md` (one Bonsai BitNet base plus a LoRA adapter per node, successor to the cognitive swarm), is marked DORMANT since 2026-06-23 with only Phase 0 (instrument) done; Phase 1 (an Emotion regressor head on MiniLM embeddings, no LLM), Phase 2 (one LoRA'd generative node) and Phase 3 never started. Four things are stale: (1) the corpora carry no quality signal: `hedged` is False on 4,085 of 4,085 voice rows, `data/judge_panel_log.jsonl` rows have no `cycle_id` to join on, `data/critic_grade_log.jsonl` has one row, so the plan's own mitigation ("train only on Critic-approved cycles") cannot be applied, and a distillation today would copy the teacher's monoculture (the 08-20 audit put form collapse at post-training alignment); (2) known-bad stretches: the 30-day outage 07-09 to 08-07 and the J-1642 voice bug live since 08-21, which mislabels some voice rows tagged `input_type=human`; (3) the assumed fine-tune tooling (QVAC or Microsoft BitNet LoRA on Bonsai) is unverified; (4) the identity fine-tune's cheapest diagnostic (audit Tier 0 item 4: re-evaluate the adapter with full, empty and absent system prompt) is in no ledger as done [INFERRED from absence in findings and jobs, not proven].

**What training is for, three different goals under one word (my analysis, offered to Travis):** speed and VRAM (Emotion and Salience emit fixed-shape vectors, a regressor replaces an LLM call; 4,089 emotion rows exist, but the plan's own rule says only freeze a node whose behaviour has stabilised, and emotion construction has been reworked since); specialisation (per-node adapters, blocked on the label gap more than on hardware); and Du's own voice (the qwen3-du path, most in tension with "think properly first"). The cheapest useful change is to make the corpora joinable to verdicts, which is J-2205.
