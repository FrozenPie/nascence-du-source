## Findings
1. **The engine reads the oldest history.** `core/udms/engine.py:129` returns `commits[:max_commits]` (450) after `git log --reverse`, so Nascence is read through 2026-08-27 to 2026-09-10 of a history running to 2026-11-06. 1,087 of 1,266 scanned code files (86%) have no temporal evidence, and the 59 of 281 post-freeze logged dossiers whose target the engine could not see all said LOW RISK. A file that does not exist (CLAUDE.md's example target `core/somatic_engine.py`; the real one is `core/somatic.py`) and `udms_query_pmi` on any unseen file also return success with 0.0 bits (F-4181, F-4182). The regime label flips with the window too: Regime II at the production window, Regime I at the newest 450, and `scan` and `battery` disagree on the same window (F-4186).
2. **What the dossier serves.** Across the 400 logged dossiers the 7,103 investigation slots are 82% CONTRACT (static imports), 16% DOCUMENTED, 1.2% STATIC, 0.4% STATE and 0.14% TEMPORAL (10 slots); the battery finds the tiered ordering no better than untiered or plain churn in most windows (F-4183). Out of sample on Nascence, plain co-change counts and churn beat every PMI or IC variant; IC_025 on counts is the best of the PMI family and beats the raw `pmi_rec` term at `core/udms/ranker.py:97-98` by 3 to 20 points (F-4193).
3. **Other UDMS defects.** doc_audit overstates rot about 2.7 times: 15% of testable mentions are broken against the 42% its concordance implies, 30% of its mentions are duplicate path tails and 46% of its "phantom" references exist (F-4184). `udms_similar_history` ranks by how many bursts of commits a file has, not when: unbanded DTW is unchanged by a circular shift, a call costs about 19 s, and it has zero real calls ever (F-4185). Three Nascence `.py` files start with a UTF-8 BOM and UDMS silently empties them, including `core/lane2.py` (62 commits, 33 importers), so three live functions show as dead (F-4187). Hygiene: of 88 "dead" functions 23 are live (15 stdlib callback methods, 4 BOM-blind, 3 alias-import-blind, 1 by-name test dispatch) and 36 of 37 "orphan" classes are used in their own file (F-4188).
4. **Nascence itself.** The hygiene review (an agent reading the code) found no wiring bug that changes behaviour: 18 superseded, 16 staged scaffolds, 6 write-only instruments, 25 plain dead, and one abandoned module, `core/world_knowledge.py` (F-4190). [INFERRED] hygiene hides about as much as it shows: a scope-aware count finds 61 more zero-caller functions, 40 of them `status()` readers, as static upper bounds (F-4189). Structure: the hot centre moved from `sensorium` to `orchestrator` and `inner_monologue_node`; the import graph is acyclic only because 35% of edges are function-scoped (counting them gives one 217-module cycle); `_think` is 3,400 lines (F-4191). Small defects: an invalid escape in `scripts/librarian.py`'s docstring, the three BOM files, one archived script that does not parse, and the CLAUDE.md example target (F-4192).
5. **Argon's cross-domain block and the GPT round.** Its arithmetic now replicates almost always; its mechanisms mostly fail on a control it did not run (F-4197). `memory_fragments.valence` is NULL on 59.8% of rows (101,599 of 169,788, including all of inner_thought, subconscious_synthesis, action and active_imagination), so the "LLM darkening" of -0.154 is a NULL-to-0 artifact; use `fragment_context.valence` or exclude NULLs (F-4194). The "coupled system" in the second-order audit is a source-mix composition effect: 0 of 21 pairs survive once the day's reading and inner share is controlled (F-4199). The ignition log's `contrib` is an integral since the previous ignition of any kind (`core/climate_engine.py:1063-1076` and `1126-1148`), reset at every fire, so it cannot reconstruct the reservoir and the "98% collinear drives" reading comes from a shared integration interval (F-4196). `subconscious_synthesis` at importance 0.9 and replay_count 0 on every row is by design (`nodes/subconscious_node.py:1469-1476` stores it as a permanent archive row); it is read at about 0.14 times its corpus share, and the usage instrument GPT said was missing already exists as `data/co_recall_ledger.jsonl` (F-4195, F-4198). `activity_events` fragment timestamps are last-touched times, so the "forward recall" is an artifact, and `replay_count` is not a recall counter (F-4200).
