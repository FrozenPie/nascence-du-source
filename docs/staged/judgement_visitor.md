## What it is
Visible to Du, and saves no judge calls (those savings belong to the docket). A correspondent, in the manner of his
penpals, who has read selected passages of his thinking and asks him to explain himself. An advisor who decides
nothing. His explanation never changes a ruling: docket rulings are blind and sealed, so the visitor cannot be argued
with or flattered into a verdict. The conversation is for Du's thinking, not for the judges.

## Why it is not "a judge"
I-0153 noted the Maxwell judgment fear (2026-08-23): an entity named a judge is the wrong frame. The visitor is a
curious reader, not a grader. The peer plan never built its challenge half; this fills it, without verdicts.

## Hard rules (proposed, for ratification)
1. No verdicts spoken or implied to Du; no docket row or ruling reaches him (D-0434).
2. Visitor questions come from his own quoted text, never from a ruling's outcome.
3. Nothing the visitor learns flows to the docket judges. One-way wall, audited with a canary string test.
4. Anti-flattery: at least one challenge per letter, graded by the existing peer stance-compliance judge; praise-only
   letters fail.
5. Du may decline or go quiet; silence is not penalised.

## Open design choices
Each has a decide entry: does Du know he is read; cadence; new peer vs existing penpal; anti-flattery mechanism.
