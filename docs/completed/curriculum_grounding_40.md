# Du's Grounding Curriculum — 40 books
*2026-06-15. Chosen to GROUND him: every title has a hard, literal referent (a knot, a candle, a tide
pool, a riverbed's hydrology) and asks him to attend to the world on its own terms. Deliberately NO
abstract philosophy, poetry, or self-reflective literature — those feed the river-metaphor monoculture;
these contest it. The Scout's Handbook is the keynote: do, make, observe, know. All public-domain
(Project Gutenberg) — fetchable by his library; exact title-resolution to be verified on load.*

**Pointed choice:** several titles give his #1 attractor — *river / water* — a real HYDROLOGICAL meaning
(Tyndall's *Forms of Water*, *Ponds and Streams*, *Sea-Beach at Ebb-Tide*, plus Twain's working-pilot
Mississippi). That's the semantic lever aimed straight at the word that's been monopolised.
